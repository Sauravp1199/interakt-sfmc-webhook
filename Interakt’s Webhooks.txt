Webhook Body – workflow_response_update:
(Note – The workflow webhook does not follow the same pattern as other webhooks with customer and message objects, it only sends the data object with the customer identifier and workflow responses )

The workflow webhook will be triggered at each step. However, if the customer replies to the 1st step, then a webhook will be triggered with only 1 question and response in data field. Then the customer replies to 2nd step, another webhook will be triggered which have 2 questions and 2 answer in the data field. Which means 1st and 2nd too. Developer will have to ensure they don’t duplicate the messages.

For incoming workflow update webhooks, the type will be as follows:
